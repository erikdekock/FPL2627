What he is good at, and what he is not

Good at

  • Finding cheap defenders early. Shaw → De Cuyper (£4.7m) in GW3 is the best transfer in the league: +49 points over eight weeks. Bogle in GW8 added +12.
  • Hits that pay. His GW8 double move (Aina → Bogle, Saka → Palmer) made +21 for a −4 hit. His GW4 hit roughly broke even (+5 for −4).
  • Fast starts. +84 against the average in the first four weeks. A first-week Bench Boost (69) set it up.
  • Growing team value. From £100.0m to £103.3m: he buys players before they rise.

Not good at

  • Captain gambles. Leaving Haaland cost him −10 (Bruno, GW6) and −18 (Palmer, GW8). His two good gambles (+20, +6) only just cover it: overall −2 against always captaining Haaland.
  • Picking his XI. 119 points left on the bench, 22 in GW6 and 21 in GW9 alone. That is the third highest in the league; we left 37.
  • Patience. Cherki and Saka lasted one week, Semenyo two. Short-term moves lost him points twice (−5, −5).
  • Using his money. £3.4m unspent: roughly the gap between a budget forward and a proven starter.

Where he can improve, and what it means for us

Where he can improve

  • Captain on the favourite unless a differential is clearly better. His two failed gambles cost 28 points, more than the gap he now has to us.
  • Start the right eleven. His 119 bench points are more than three times the 36 he trails Jason by.
  • Spend the £3.4m. Ndiaye (£5.8m) and O'Shea (£4.0m) on his bench could become one strong starter.
  • Hold players for three weeks before judging them. His one-week moves were his weakest.

What he must hold steady

  • His defensive scouting: De Cuyper, Bogle, Calafiori and Raya give him a strong, cheap back line.
  • Disciplined hits: only when two moves clearly beat −4, as in GW8.
  • Value growth: £103.3m gives him the most room in the league for a Wildcard rebuild.
  • A shared core with the leader: nine of his fifteen are also Jason's, so he does not drop further when the template scores.

His opportunities

  • Wildcard and Free Hit still in hand. He can rebuild for the Christmas schedule and still Free Hit a blank or double week. Jason has these too; we do not.
  • Differentials to catch Jason. With nine players in common he can only gain on Jason through the other six. Expect bolder picks.
  • Cash plus team value. £3.4m and the highest value means a Wildcard could fit Haaland, Palmer and another premium.
  • Fixtures. Bournemouth (Tavernier, Evanilson) have NFO, BHA and HUL at home in the next four weeks.

What it means for us

  • He is 9 behind us with two chips more. His Wildcard week is the week to watch: if he plays it into a good run of fixtures, he can pass us in one or two weeks.
  • Shared players cancel out. We share five (Haaland, De Cuyper, Groß, Tavernier, Bogle). Our points on him come from Isak, Mbeumo, Ødegaard, Barry and our Leeds/Brighton defenders.
  • His threats to us: Palmer, Rogers, Evanilson, Calvert-Lewin and Raya, which none of our fifteen cover.
  • Captaincy: he picked Haaland in 5 of 10 weeks. When he deviates he usually loses, so a shield captain against him is cheap.
