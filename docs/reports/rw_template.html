What he is good at, and what he is not

Good at

  • Spotting value forwards and defenders. Watkins → Calvert-Lewin (GW2) made +38 and Cash → De Cuyper (GW5) +32: two of the best transfers in the league.
  • Discipline. Zero hits all season and one transfer a week. His seven normal transfers made +76 between them.
  • Consistency. Above the average every week since GW3: eight in a row, the longest run in the league. Nobody else has more than five.
  • A strong Wildcard. His GW4 rebuild brought in Tavernier (+33 against the player he replaced) and Gvardiol (+17).

Not good at

  • The armband. Leaving Haaland cost him 50 points: Cunha in GW2 (−8), Gakpo in GW4 (−16) and Cherki in GW7, who did not play, so vice Gvardiol scored 0 (−26).
  • Chip timing. All three used chips gave modest returns: Triple Captain +9, the Wildcard week 73 (+4 on the average), the Free Hit 57 (+2 on the average).
  • A rich bench. Raya (£6.2m), Cherki (£7.9m) and João Pedro (£7.6m) were on his GW9 bench: £21.7m doing nothing.
  • Using his money. £4.7m unspent, the second most in the league after Richard.

Where he can improve, and what it means for us

Where he can improve

  • Captain Haaland by default. Simply doing that would have given him 50 more points, enough to move from ninth to fifth.
  • Check the captain's minutes. GW7 went to a player who did not play. A vice-captain who reliably starts is a cheap insurance.
  • Put the money on the pitch. £4.7m in the bank plus £21.7m on the bench can become two strong starters in one or two moves.
  • Save the Bench Boost for a double gameweek, when his expensive bench players play twice.

What he must hold steady

  • One transfer a week, no hits. It works: +76 from seven transfers.
  • His scouting of cheap starters: Calvert-Lewin (£6.0m), De Cuyper (£4.8m), Van Hecke and Konsa give him a strong core for little money.
  • The weekly rhythm. Eight weeks above average is the habit that climbs ranks over a season.
  • A team value that keeps rising: from £99.7m in GW3 to £102.5m.

His opportunities

  • Captaincy alone is worth about 5 points a week on his record so far. That is the cheapest climb in the league.
  • Bench Boost in hand. With Raya, Cherki and João Pedro on the bench it could be one of the strongest boosts in the league, if he picks a double week.
  • Cash. £4.7m lets him buy almost any player next week without selling a starter.
  • Fixtures. Bournemouth (Tavernier, Kluivert) have NFO, BHA and HUL at home in the next four weeks.

What it means for us

  • Not a title threat yet: 68 behind us with only the Bench Boost left. His steady scoring makes him the one to watch from the bottom half.
  • Shared players cancel out. We share five: Haaland, De Cuyper, Groß, Tavernier and Tzolakis.
  • His differentials against us: Raya, Van Hecke, Konsa, Gvardiol, Calvert-Lewin and Kluivert.
  • For our title race he matters little: he only helps us in weeks he outscores Jason, and with five shared players that will be rare.
