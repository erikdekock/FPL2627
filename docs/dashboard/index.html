Transfers in the league

Every transfer from GW1 until now. A transfer is judged over the weeks the manager kept the new player: the new player's points minus the sold player's points in exactly those weeks. Positive is a good swap. A hit (−4) is split over that week's transfers. Free Hit weeks do not count, because the old team comes back. On a Wildcard the API pairs out and in at random, so those transfers are listed but do not count in the manager totals.
