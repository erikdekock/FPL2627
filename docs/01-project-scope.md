## 1. Mission & objectives

**Mission:** Compete seriously in Fantasy Premier League 2026/27 with a structured, data-informed, low-stress process — one focused session per gameweek, decisions logged, lessons compounding.

**Objectives (to be locked in Setup Chat #2):**
- Target overall rank — pick a tier: Top 1M (solid), Top 250k (good), Top 100k (strong for a first structured season), Top 10k (elite, requires more risk).
- Mini-league goals: which leagues, who are the rivals to beat.
- Secondary: build a reusable playbook so season 2 starts from a higher floor.

**Operating principles:**
1. Process over outcome — judge decisions on reasoning, not one week's variance.
2. Plan 4–6 gameweeks ahead; never make a transfer that only makes sense for one week (unless it's a chip week).
3. No rage transfers. Post-deadline emotions get logged, not acted on.
4. Hits (-4) only with explicit expected-value reasoning.
5. Bias guardrail: Erik is an Arsenal fan and Arsenal are the reigning champions. Every Arsenal pick must pass the "would I buy this player at this price if he played for Aston Villa?" test.

## 3. Rules & scoring — 2025/26 baseline, confirm at launch

⚠️ **FPL has NOT gone live for 2026/27 yet** (as of 13 July). Launch expected after the World Cup final (19 July); last season launched ~1 month before GW1. Everything below is the 2025/26 ruleset as working baseline. **First job at launch: rules audit → update this section + Notion "Rules Reference".**

**Squad & budget:** £100.0m · 15 players (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD) · max 3 per club.

**Scoring core (25/26):**
- Appearance 1–2 pts · Goals: GK/DEF 6, MID 5, FWD 4 · Assist 3 (simplified/broader definition since 25/26) · Clean sheet: GK/DEF 4, MID 1.
- **Defensive contribution (new in 25/26):** DEF earn 2 pts at 10+ CBIT (clearances+blocks+interceptions+tackles) in a match; MID/FWD earn 2 pts at 12+ CBIRT (adds ball recoveries). This quietly made holding midfielders and blocks-machine centre-backs viable — factor into cheap-defence strategy.
- Bonus: 3/2/1 per match via BPS (Opta) · Saves 1 per 3 · Pen save 5 · Goals conceded −1 per 2 (GK/DEF) · Cards −1/−3 · Pen miss −2 · OG −2.

**Transfers:** 1 free transfer per GW, bankable up to 5. Extra transfers −4 each.

**Chips (25/26 baseline):** TWO of each — Wildcard ×2, Free Hit ×2, Bench Boost ×2, Triple Captain ×2. One set per half-season; first-half set expires at the GW19 deadline. ❓ Confirm at launch whether this repeats for 26/27.

**Prices & team value:** ±£0.1m moves driven by net transfer volume; you keep only 50% of any rise on selling (buy 6.0 → price 6.4 → sell at 6.2). Team value is a mid-season weapon, not an early obsession.

**Launch audit checklist:** chip set & deadlines · DEFCON thresholds · assist definition · any new chip/feature · price list release → then rebuild squad drafts.

## 4. Strategy framework — the questions we must answer

Strategy lives on three layers. Every layer has fixed questions; answers get documented in Notion.

**Layer 1 — Season strategy (set pre-season, revisit monthly):**
- What rank are we targeting, and therefore what risk posture? (Top 100k+ = mostly template with picked spots; Top 10k = sustained differential risk.)
- Squad structure: how many premiums (players ≥ £10m) and where? Big at the back or budget defence + DEFCON picks? Playing 3-4-3, 3-5-2, 4-4-2?
- Budget allocation per line (GK/DEF/MID/FWD) — decide once prices are out.
- Template vs differential ratio: how much of the "effective ownership top-10" do we hold vs fade?
- Which teams do we structurally want coverage of (fixtures, style, minutes security)?

**Layer 2 — Rolling 4–6 GW plan (updated weekly):**
- Which fixture swings are coming — who to board/exit and in which GW?
- Transfer path: what do the next 3 moves look like if nothing changes? How many FTs banked?
- Blank/Double GW radar: what are cup rounds doing to the schedule?
- Watchlist: who's 1–2 good performances away from buying?

**Layer 3 — Gameweek execution (the weekly chat, §6):**
- Transfers, captain, XI/bench order, chip check.

**Player evaluation criteria (the checklist for any pick):**
1. Minutes security — is he nailed? (rotation risk, new-manager risk, Europe congestion)
2. Underlying numbers — xG, xA, xGI per 90; for defenders also CS odds + DEFCON floor.
3. Fixtures next 4–6 (attack- and defence-adjusted, not just raw FDR).
4. Set pieces & penalties — yes/no, and order in the pecking.
5. Price value — projected points per £m vs alternatives in slot.
6. Ownership/EO — is this a shield (high EO) or a sword (differential <10%)?
7. Team context — new signings around him, manager style, injury cluster.

**Key concepts glossary (for the Notion reference page):** template, effective ownership (EO), differential, FDR, xGI, nailedness, price anchoring, team value (TV), rank protection vs rank chasing, BGW/DGW.

## 5. Chip strategy framework

Chips are the biggest single rank-swing lever in the game. Rules:

1. **Every chip gets a planned window before the season starts** (Chip Planner in Notion). Windows shift; chips never fire reactively without an EV note.
2. **Triple Captain & Bench Boost** want Double Gameweeks — or, if the two-per-half format returns and forces first-half usage, an elite home fixture cluster.
3. **Free Hit** is for Blank Gameweeks or one-off fixture-hell weeks (or a punt week in a DGW).
4. **Wildcard 1** classic window: GW4–8 — after new-manager roles settle (extra relevant this season with 8 new bosses) and often around the first international break (post-GW5 this year).
5. **Wildcard 2** sets up the spring chip block around BGW/DGWs created by FA Cup R5/QF and the EFL Cup final. Track via Ben Crellin's planner from ~January.
6. Weekly chat always contains one line: *"Chip check: use / hold, because…"*

## 6. Weekly operating rhythm — what we actually do every week

**Cadence:** one chat per gameweek inside the Claude project, named `GW01 — 21/22 Aug`, `GW02 — …`. Open it Friday evening or Saturday morning NL time (after press conferences), well before the deadline. Deadline = 90 min before the GW's first kickoff, shown in UK time (NL = UK +1h).

**Erik brings (2 min):** squad screenshot (or just team ID once set — Claude can pull picks live), bank + FTs, current rank, any gut feelings/doubts.

**Claude's weekly checklist (the standing agenda):**
1. **Pull live data** — FPL API (prices, ownership, flags) + search: press-conference/injury news, price changes overnight, predicted lineups.
2. **Last GW in 2 minutes** — points vs average, rank move, one lesson → log.
3. **Fixture scan** — this GW + next 4, for our squad and watchlist.
4. **Transfer decision** — recommendation A (clear, with reasoning) + one alternative B; hold-the-FT is always a candidate; hit calculus explicit.
5. **Captain & vice** — EO-aware (when do we shield, when do we swing).
6. **XI + bench order** — autosub logic considered.
7. **Chip check** — one line, per §5.
8. **Decision log → Notion Gameweek Log** — one row, 60 seconds.

**Time budget:** 30–45 min/week in-season. Monthly (every 4–6 GWs): a short review chat — rank trajectory, what the log says about our decision quality, Layer-1 adjustments.

## 7. Data sources & tools

**Official / live data:**
- fantasy.premierleague.com + app — the game itself.
- **FPL API (free, public, no auth)** — Claude pulls these directly in weekly chats:
  - `https://fantasy.premierleague.com/api/bootstrap-static/` — all players, prices, ownership, form, flags, teams, GW deadlines
  - `https://fantasy.premierleague.com/api/fixtures/` — full fixture list + difficulty
  - `https://fantasy.premierleague.com/api/element-summary/{player_id}/` — per-player history & upcoming
  - `https://fantasy.premierleague.com/api/entry/{team_id}/` and `.../event/{gw}/picks/` — Erik's public team & picks once team ID is saved
- The Scout (official FPL content on premierleague.com) — solid for rule announcements & DEFCON data.

**Core external stack (all have free tiers):**
- **Fantasy Football Scout** — articles, community, launch-news speed.
- **Fantasy Football Hub** — Ben Crellin's planner & FPL calendar (THE blank/double gameweek resource), Opta stats, chip guides.
- **Fantasy Football Fix** — price-change predictions, points projections, rotation planner.
- **LiveFPL** (livefpl.net) — live rank, effective ownership of the top 10k. The EO reference.
- **Premier Fantasy Tools** — press-conference summaries (weekly must-read), fixture ticker, season planner.
- **fpl.page / FPL Focal** — DEFCON tracking, live bonus.
- **Premier Injuries** — injury table with return dates.
- **r/FantasyPL** — sentiment radar (what the template is about to do).
- Narrative layer (optional): Let's Talk FPL, FPL Harry (YouTube); Planet FPL, Always Cheating (podcasts).

**Source hygiene:** the FPL web is full of AI-generated slop sites that confidently describe rules that don't exist (already spotted one claiming the Assistant Manager chip is live in 26/27). Rule: game rules only from premierleague.com; anything surprising gets a second source.

**Subscription decision:** start free. After ~GW8, evaluate whether one paid tool (Hub or Fix) earns its cost. Log in Notion.

## 11. Guardrails / out of scope
