## 2. Recruitment: the player-type taxonomy

Every squad slot belongs to an archetype. Archetypes define what a player must deliver, what he costs, when we buy and sell him, and how many we carry. A transfer that can't name its archetype doesn't happen.

**A · De Anker (premium captain) — £11.5m+ · exactly 2 slots.**
Haaland (15.5), Bruno (12.0). Job: captaincy floor and ceiling; never benched, never rotated out by us. Buy: pre-season. Sell: only on injury or structural role collapse — an Anker sale is a wildcard-level event. This flat market has just two of them, which makes the captaincy meta unusually concentrated: owning both is itself a shield.

**B · De Value-Ster — £6.5–9.5m · 3–4 slots.**
The engine room of this season: the price list's value mass sits exactly here. Current: Watkins (8.0), Szoboszlai (7.0). Market: Semenyo (8.5), Palmer (9.5), Gyökeres (7.5), Isak (9.0), João Pedro (7.5). Job: 5+ points per week at mid-price. Buy: on fixture swings and minutes evidence. Sell: when a 4–6 GW fixture wall arrives or the role degrades. This band is where most transfers of the season will happen.

**C · De Vloerspeler (set pieces / DefCon / penalties) — £5.5–6.5m · 2–3 slots.**
Stach (6.0, corners + DefCon at ~2% owned), Ndiaye (6.0, pens — under review), Caicedo-type DMs. Job: raise the weekly floor — a 2-point DefCon baseline plus dead-ball upside funds the Ankers. Buy: on confirmed set-piece duty. Sell: the moment the duty moves — that *is* the exit condition.

**D · De Regel-Trade — any price · 1–2 slots.**
Players mispriced because the *rules* changed faster than the price list: attacking full-backs and keepers under the new BPS (Muñoz, 5.5), position reclassifications (11 this season), year-two DefCon accumulators. Job: exploit rule-lag before the market reprices. Buy: at launch/rule-change. Sell: when ownership catches up — the edge is temporary by definition.

**E · De Enabler — £4.0–4.5m · 3–4 slots (incl. bench).**
Davis (4.0, the community-consensus pick: Ipswich open home to Sunderland). Job: play actual minutes for minimal money; insurance that starts when needed. Buy: promoted-club starters with soft openers. Sell: rarely — churn here wastes transfers. Bench GK may be a genuine non-player.

**F · De Punt — usually <£6.5m and <10% owned · max 1–2 slots, hard cap.**
The "cheap gambles" that win mini-leagues: post-launch signings at near-zero ownership (the António Silva case), January entrants, promoted-team attackers on pens. Sourced exclusively through the scout radar + five-question filter. Every Punt carries a written thesis and exit; a Punt without either is a mood, and rule 9 blocks it. This is where the aggressive posture lives cheaply.

**G · De Kalender-Speler — any price · overlay, not a slot.**
The structural insight of §1.2: **Spurs, Newcastle, Forest and Chelsea attackers play once a week all season** — no Thursday legs, no MD6-in-17-days stretches, predictable XIs. Gibbs-White, Bruno Guimarães-types, Palmer. Weight this overlay upward in Phases 3 and 5, when the nine European clubs drown in fixtures and these four swim.

**Anti-archetype · Het Congestie-Risico — never buy.**
Squad players (not nailed starters) at the nine European clubs, and anyone at a new-manager club without minutes evidence. The intersection — a rotation-prone attacker at, say, European Palace under a new boss — is the single worst purchase profile of this season.

**Squad formula check (current 15):** 2 Ankers ✓ · Value-Sterren: Watkins, Szoboszlai (2 — light, by design until the market settles) · Vloer: Stach, Ndiaye (+Collins/Senesi as defensive floors) ✓ · Regel-Trade: Muñoz ✓ · Enablers: Davis, Keane, 2 bench slots ✓ · Punt: 0 (deliberate for GW1; first Punt window is Phase 2) · Kalender-overlay: Senesi (Spurs) ✓.

## 3. The six phases — full detail

### FASE 1 · BEWIJS — GW1–5 · 21 Aug – 20 Sep
**Calendar inside it:** window open through GW1–2 (closes 1 Sep) · European league phases start mid-Sep (MD1 lands around GW4/5 — first real rotation signals at the nine) · no international football until 21 Sep · Brighton already playing Thursday qualifiers.
**Who's fresh, who isn't:** deep World Cup runners (Spain, Argentina, England, France) ease in — expect minute management through GW3. Non-WC and early-exit players are at full throttle. Villa carry Super Cup legs (played 12 Aug).
**Fixture themes (verified anchors):** Arsenal open v Coventry (H) · Utd's soft ladder: Hull (A), Ipswich (H), Everton (A) — one top-half opponent in seven · Haaland H v Coventry in GW3 · Ipswich H v Sunderland GW1 (the Davis pick) · Fulham–Chelsea closes GW1 on Monday.
**Sub-goals (phase exit):** ≤3 free transfers used · zero hits · minutes-tag (Nailed/Likely/50-50/Doubt) verified for all 20 clubs' key assets · rival archetypes drafted from their GW1–5 behaviour · E2 early-warning checked at GW3 in writing.
**Sub-strategy:** transfers only for availability, never form · captaincy strictly from the pool (Haaland default; Bruno GW2 H Ipswich is the pool alternative) · watch deadline-day entrants (Silva-class) getting priced into the game GW3+ · log discipline from day one — the calibration dataset starts now.
**Key risk:** the patience edge inverting (obvious new-manager picks simply working). The check is scheduled, written, and honest.

### FASE 2 · RESET & STRUCTUUR — break + GW6–10 · 21 Sep – 7 Nov
**Calendar:** the three-week break — internationals play up to FOUR matches, so European internationals return leggy for GW6 (9–11 Oct) · UCL/UEL MDs roll through Oct · second break after GW10 (9–17 Nov).
**The set piece of the season:** **Q1 review 21–24 Sep** (start of the break, maximum build time): grade every Phase-1 assumption, set rival archetypes, then **build the Wildcard squad during the break** on five gameweeks of real evidence and fire **WC1 in GW6**. **TC1 in GW7 — Haaland home to Ipswich** — on a fresh wildcard squad. **BB1 in the best all-15 fixture week of GW8–12**, selected at Q1 with the ticker; before December congestion, after the WC has made all fifteen playable.
**Sub-goals:** core-11 locked by GW8 · three of the four first-half chips fired or hard-scheduled · rank gate: trending toward ~top 500k · first Punt slot opened if the scout radar has produced a filtered candidate.
**Sub-strategy:** the WC build weights archetype B heavily (the value band will have revealed its winners), adds the first F-Punt, and applies the G-overlay for the winter ahead. Post-break captaincy: prefer players whose national teams played two, not four, matches.

### FASE 3 · CONGESTIE & DE CHIP-KLIF — GW11–19 · 21 Nov – 2/3 Jan
**Calendar:** UCL MD5 (26 Nov) and MD6 (10 Dec) · two midweek PL rounds · festive schedule with the 60-hour rule · **the cliff: GW19 deadline, Sat 2 Jan 13:30 GMT — every unfired first-half chip evaporates.**
**Who suffers:** the nine European clubs hit peak rotation — this is when pre-planned G-archetype exposure (Spurs/Newcastle/Forest/Chelsea + Everton/Leeds/Brentford/Fulham assets) pays. Managers rest players around UCL MD6 and festive triples.
**Sub-goals:** zero chips expired unused · rotation losses (points on bench / benched-by-manager) at or below what the log shows for rivals · **Q2 review mid-Dec:** rank gate ~top 150k, posture check, first calibration verdict (two named biases).
**Sub-strategy:** **FH1's planned burn: GW17–18 if still alive** — never GW19 (blocks FH2 in GW20) · captaincy shifts rotation-proof: the Ankers still, but verify midweek minutes before every armband · transfer tempo rises to the weekly FT, spent on fixture swings and congestion-dodging · Monday/festive late kickoffs get the blind-lineup discount every single week.

### FASE 4 · WINTERHERBOUW — GW20–26 · Jan – Feb
**Calendar:** January window (entrants at 0% ownership — Punt season) · UCL MD7/8 late Jan · knockout play-offs 18/25 Feb · EFL Cup final late Feb/early Mar creates the first blank risk for finalists · second chip set unlocks at GW20.
**The set piece:** **WC2 late Jan/early Feb**, built on Ben Crellin's blank/double map — which hardens in January — with one purpose: a squad shaped for the spring chip block. Every WC2 pick answers "how many fixtures does he have in GW28–34?"
**Sub-goals:** WC2 fired with a written double/blank rationale · ≤2 active Punts, each graded · G-overlay re-weighted (cup runs change who's congested).
**Sub-strategy:** January entrants get the scout-radar fast-track but the same five-question filter · sell archetype-D trades whose ownership has caught up · league check: if behind the rival, this is the phase to open leverage (differential captaincy) because chips give cover.

### FASE 5 · HET LENTEBLOK — GW27–33 · Mar – Apr
**Calendar:** March break 22–30 Mar (Nations League QFs + Euro 2028 qualifying starts — internationals travel again) · FA Cup R5/QF and UCL R16/QF carve the league schedule → **the blank and double gameweeks live here** · one midweek PL round.
**The set piece — three chips, three structural weeks:** **FH2 on the biggest blank** (field an XI while half the league fields six) · **BB2 on the biggest double** (historically the highest-yield chip in the game) · **TC2 on an Anker with two home-leaning fixtures in one GW.** Exact GWs come from Crellin's map at Q3 (early Mar); the plan is the shape, the map fills the numbers.
**Sub-goals:** all three chips land in structural weeks, none reactive · rank gate ~top 50k at Q3 · bench built double-aware (autosubs matter double in DGWs).
**Sub-strategy:** ownership of double-fixture teams beats form · beware post-March-break GW (same logic as GW6: internationals return late) · rival chip tracking becomes decisive — if the rival banks his BB2 for the same double, our week is shield; if he's burned everything, our chips are pure sword.

### FASE 6 · EINDSPEL — GW34–38 · May, final day Sun 30 May
**Calendar quirk that decides titles:** UCL semis 29 Apr/6 May and the **final on 26 May — four days before GW38.** Any English finalist (five candidates) will rotate league games in GW36–38. Never captain, and ideally don't field, a UCL-finalist's star in the last rounds without confirmed team news. FA Cup final mid-May does the same in miniature.
**Sub-strategy:** **league above everything** — every decision graded against the rival, not the field: ahead → mirror his captain, kill variance, force him to need luck; behind → maximum leverage, captaincy first, and the last Punts get their stage · GW38 is all-simultaneous, so no late-information games — pure pre-commitment.
**Sub-goals:** the only two that matter: league won · 38/38 log rows for the season-two engine.
