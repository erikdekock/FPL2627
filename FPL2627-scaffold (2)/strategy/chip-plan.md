# Chip Master Plan — FPL 2026/27

## Confirmed rules (official, 20 Jul 2026)

Two sets of four chips (Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain, Bench Boost) — 8 total. First set **expires at the GW19 deadline, 13:30 GMT Sat 2 Jan 2027**; no carry-over. One chip per Gameweek. Free Hit cannot be played in GW1; FH1 in GW19 blocks FH2 in GW20.

No chip fires without a written rationale in this file *before* the deadline. Reactive chip use is banned; a chip proposal mid-week goes through one night's sleep.
